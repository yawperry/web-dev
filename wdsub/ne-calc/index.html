nash equilibrium calculator

This applet takes a two-player game in normal form and computes pure-strategy Nash Equilibria using Iterated Elimination of Dominated Strategies [IEDS].

To user the calculator, begin by entering the number of pure strategies avaliable to player 1 and the number of pure strategies avaliable to player 2. Then press go. on the next screen, enter the payoff pairs for wach outcome and press compute.
