With head held high and heart full of grace,
I walk the path of life with confidence in my pace.
No longer do doubts and fears hold me back,
For I have learned to trust in myself and my track.
I know my worth and I know my power,
I am strong and capable in every hour.
I will not let others dictate my fate,
For I am the master of my own state.
I will not shrink or fade away, I will shine bright like the sun’s rays.
I will not let insecurities consume,
For I am worthy of love and success, it’s my birthright to assume.
So let me be bold, let me be true, For I am a force to be reckoned with, this much is true.
I am confident and I will soar,
For I am me, and that is something to adore.
